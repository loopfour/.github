## About

This is the official GitHub home of loopfour.ai.

loopfour.ai is a workflow automation platform for finance, built on a simple idea: workflow automation without AI hallucinations. Finance teams get deterministic, auditable automation for accounts receivable, accounts payable, month-end close, and revenue recognition, with people stepping in only to approve the exceptions.

We are the connective tissue for financial data. loopfour.ai moves financial data across business systems at scale, syncing CRMs, billing systems, accounting platforms, ERPs, and data warehouses without fragile custom integrations or engineering overhead.

We are a Y Combinator company (W23) based in San Francisco, and we are hiring.
